# Animation library

AnimationLibrary_Godot_Standard.gltf/bin — Quaternius **Universal Animation
Library** (free version), CC0 1.0. Obtained from the mirror
https://github.com/J-Ponzo/gltf-universal-animation-library (as distributed
at https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library).
Clips are retargeted from the Rigify DEF-rig onto the Mixamo-rigged bodies
at bake time by tools/retarget.gd (world-space transfer with a
Rigify->Mixamo bone map).
